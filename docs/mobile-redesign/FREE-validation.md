## Changes and experience

- The Free estimator starts with compact allowance and mode controls, followed by inputs. At the matching 390-pixel fixture viewport, the first input begins roughly 240 pixels earlier. Optional guidance is under Help, with the existing tutorial still mounted and discoverable. Incomplete inputs show neutral guidance before submission; inline errors and consequential warnings remain visible.
- The compact action area distinguishes reviewing incomplete inputs, calculating and saving, and viewing a saved result. Its measured height coordinates clearance with the existing bottom bar, viewport observer and keyboard input accessory. Units, allowance, correction cost/window, unsaved state and uncertain outcomes remain explicit.
- Saved history puts search and records first. Filters and Help fold without losing values or the active-filter summary. Saved label, version, date and monetary units remain readable. Saved-result sheets now use the completed receipt's label/revision even after an unsaved rename; the desktop Free sheet receives this correctness fix without a layout change.
- Free account groups summarize existing personal, factory, security and device editors. Editors stay mounted, dirty state remains visible, and invalid or delayed failure feedback keeps/reopens the relevant group. Deletion consequences and server safeguards are preserved; Cancel restores mobile focus.
- Free billing uses actual organization/access facts and explicit unavailable, pending, suspended and ended states. A verified mounted paid upgrade displays its paid unlimited access correctly. Plans use the existing commercial receipt policy: pilot payment receipt PDFs, with no GST tax invoice promise. Price, tax arithmetic and payment behavior are unchanged.
- Free customer support makes introduction/history optional, keeps promised updates visible and provides a Reply focus shortcut. Closing intake retains the mounted memory-only draft. Synchronous Free-only guards prevent same-task duplicate create/reply requests while preserving the original retry payload and key. Paid and founder support remain unchanged.
- Plans and entry/recovery screens use compact mobile headings, discoverable details and 44-pixel controls. Free signup retains supplied referral values and shows password rules and errors. Back to Estimator goes to the actual `/free` route. New presentation strings have English, Hindi and Gujarati parity.
- Full text-resize checks found defects hidden by document-level overflow checks. Free labels, fields and actions now grow/wrap with their text; short unit gutters scale with font size, and long `paise/pick`/`kg/100m` units flow below full-width inputs. Auth phone-prefix clearance, account action/badge bounds, payment-summary wrapping and Free receipt-link touch targets are corrected. Saved-history search/actions also grow at 200% text, and its opened guide plus the estimator invitation stack their title and launcher to prevent overlap. These are responsive presentation fixes; handlers and numerical values are unchanged.

The Free quota/receipt/transaction hook, costing engine, paid live calculations, prices, inventory/payroll rules, authentication/security/privacy behavior and deletion rules retain their existing contracts. Free still has no paid Saved qualities and no durable local draft recovery, including a mounted in-place upgraded session.
