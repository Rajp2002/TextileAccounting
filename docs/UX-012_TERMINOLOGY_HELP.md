# Limited-Pilot Terminology Help

This is the product-owner-approved UX-012 baseline for the limited pilot. Actual factory-user comprehension validation remains an open pilot action.

- **Loom**: one physical weaving loom.
- **Metres Produced This Shift**: shift output, not a cumulative **Counter Reading**.
- **Fabric Quality** (first use), then **Quality**: fabric construction/specification, not a grade, Market Run, or Party Order.
- **Market Run**: production for market/Godown Stock without a committed buyer.
- **Party Order**: a buyer-backed production commitment.
- **Cost Estimate**: internal cost calculation. **Quotation**: Party-facing price proposal.
- **Party**: the buyer/customer account. **Party Ledger** shows sales, receipts, adjustments, and **Amount Due**.
- **Godown Stock**, **Stock Inward**, and **Stock Lot** describe fabric stock and its traceable entry/grouping.
- **Delivery Challan** on first use, then **Challan**: the document accompanying a Dispatch; it is not an invoice or receipt.
- **Sale**, **Dispatch**, and **Party Receipt Confirmation** are separate events. Keep **Metres Sold**, **Metres Sent**, and **Metres Received** separate.
- **Worker** in English and **Karigar / કારીગર** in applicable Gujarati/local context.
- **Piece-rate Worker** is paid from production; **Salaried Worker** is paid from salary/day/Hajiri.
- **Worker Hisaab**: payroll close. **Upaad**, **Kharchi**, Hisaab payout, **Inam**, and **Kapaat** must remain separate.
- **Hajiri**: attendance used for attendance-based pay.
- **Warp Yarn** runs lengthwise; **Weft Yarn** runs across the fabric. Confirm local terms during the pilot.
- **Taka** is provisional for the physical fabric unit and is a pilot-validation priority.
- Use `m` beside values and “metres” in prose. `kg/100m` is yarn consumption; `m/kg` is yield. They are inverse measures.
