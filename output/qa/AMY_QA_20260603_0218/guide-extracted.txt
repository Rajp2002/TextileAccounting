1. QA Mission And Pass/Fail Rules
This QA pass is meant to simulate a new factory owner adopting Weavly from scratch while Raj is away. The intern should 
validate every important user journey, create realistic records, intentionally test edge cases, and record enough evidence so 
Raj can review the findings later without needing to ask what happened.
Severity Definition Examples
Blocker A core workflow cannot be completed or 
data is corrupted.
Cannot create sale, cannot dispatch, cannot 
log production, wrong stock balance after 
receipt, auth bypass.
High Workflow completes but user trust or 
business accuracy is at risk.
Commercial balance wrong, wrong role 
copy, duplicate logs, mobile button 
inaccessible, public tracking misleading.
Medium Workflow is usable but confusing, slow, or 
missing validation.
Unclear error copy, missing empty state, 
awkward navigation, non-critical console 
warnings.
Low Polish issue. Spacing, typo, minor alignment, cosmetic 
inconsistency.
  ☐ A workflow is not considered passed until the tester confirms the created/changed data appears in the expected 
downstream page.
  ☐ For every bug, capture the exact route, role, steps, expected result, actual result, screenshot/video timestamp, and 
whether refresh/retry changed the outcome.
  ☐ If a workflow involves money, meters, takas, wages, or receivables, write down the before and after numbers.
  ☐ If a workflow seems to pass but the browser console shows red errors or repeated warnings, log it as at least Medium 
unless clearly harmless.

5.1 Public Pages And Auth Smoke
Goal: Confirm all public and auth pages load, show correct copy, and do not expose private factory data.
Steps:
1. Open landing page `/` while logged out.
2. Open `/login`, `/signup`, `/join`, `/forgot-password`, `/reset-password`, `/terms`, `/privacy`, `/refund-policy`.
3. Open an invalid tracking URL such as `/track/not-a-real-token`.
4. While logged out, directly open `/owner/today` and `/supervisor/dashboard`.
Expected result:
  ☐ All public pages load nonblank with expected copy.
  ☐ Invalid tracking URL shows a clear link-not-found state.
  ☐ Protected owner/supervisor routes redirect to login or an auth guard.
  ☐ No private factory data appears while logged out.
