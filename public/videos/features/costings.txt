00:00:00.000 — Find and inspect a saved costing
Search for the demo fabric and open its saved cost breakdown. Review the original production cost, selling price, margin and quantity.

00:00:18.875 — Reuse a costing and change assumptions
Choose Use to reopen the saved snapshot. Change the weaving rate to Rs 16/m and target margin to 20%, then inspect the new live result.

00:00:42.042 — Save a new version
Save New Version creates costing v2 while preserving v1 and its original numbers.

00:00:52.875 — Select two versions
Enable Compare, open the fabric's history, and select v1 and v2 for comparison.

00:01:15.000 — Explain the difference
Open Compare Costings to inspect the differences in cost components, selling price, margin and profit.

00:01:31.708 — Export a cost sheet or customer quotation
Preview and download the internal cost sheet. Switch to Customer Quotation, enter a demo buyer and terms, and download a quotation that hides internal costs and margins. Nothing is sent to a customer.
